# Improvement-plan contract

For every proposal record: skill ID, exact source and active path, file(s), proposed change, evidence (audit field plus path/line), inference, expected benefit, risks, validation, rollback, status, and dissent. Keep global decisions in one section; link rather than repeat them. `needs-owner-decision` is required for deletion, merging divergent implementations, scope/authority changes, dependencies, credentials, or installation.
