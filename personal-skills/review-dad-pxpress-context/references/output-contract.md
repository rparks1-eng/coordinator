# PXpress context brief contract

Return a compact brief with these sections.

## Intake summary

- Messages reviewed and unseen messages selected
- Visible date range, if available
- Links and images inspected, skipped, or inaccessible
- Confidence and collection limitations

## Confirmed direction

List evidence-backed facts and decisions. Each item includes one or more evidence labels such as `M03` or `M12 + example.com`. Keep message wording paraphrased.

## Proposed interpretation

Clearly mark conclusions that are inferred rather than stated. Explain the evidence behind each inference.

## Recommended next steps

Order actions by value and readiness. For each action, include the owner or department when known, required inputs, suggested verification, and whether approval is required before execution.

## Open questions and risks

List contradictions, missing decisions, unsafe or inaccessible sources, and anything that could materially change the recommendation.

## Privacy note

Confirm that no message was sent or modified, raw conversation content was not written to the PXpress repository, and whether the checkpoint was committed or left unchanged.
